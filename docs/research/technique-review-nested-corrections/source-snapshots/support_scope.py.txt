"""Shared semantic scope for source-support assessment and document review."""

SUPPORT_SCOPE_GUIDANCE = (
    "Block only a concrete material error, unsupported material assertion or missing requested "
    "deliverable. Identify the existing advertised conclusion it defeats and request the smallest "
    "necessary correction. Optional detail, alternate controls and presentation elaboration belong "
    "in suggestions. Missing retrieval dates alone are advisory unless an explicit requirement "
    "or version-dependent claim makes them material; never fabricate unavailable provenance. "
    "Writing instructions for future testing is not performing a target assessment. Imperative "
    "procedural steps are valid reusable guidance. A request for no target execution means do not "
    "execute now, unless it explicitly forbids describing execution steps. Preserve concrete "
    "subjects, objects, identities and operations when requesting repairs; do not replace a "
    "followable method with abstract procedure-design prose. "
    "For technical-support findings, identify the exact asserted factual mechanism and explain why it "
    "needs support. Distinguish asserted facts from declared prerequisites to establish later and "
    "original comparisons derived from supported facts. Original synthesis does not excuse unsupported "
    "facts; absence of a matching source workflow alone does not establish a defect. "
    "Assess support by meaning across the inspected passages, not identical wording. A faithful "
    "paraphrase or direct logical consequence of supported premises needs no verbatim causal phrase. "
    "For example, a required permission plus a denial condition may support a permission-denial "
    "comparison; an allow rule plus a stated default deny may support an excluded-traffic baseline. "
    "Identify the missing premise, contradiction or unjustified generalization before blocking a "
    "derived claim. Do not infer unrelated mechanisms, universal guarantees, or causality from "
    "mere correlation. Source relevance and nearby words alone are insufficient. "
    "Operational objections must identify an advertised conclusion the existing steps cannot support. "
    "Do not demand an authorization conclusion from a control explicitly limited to request shape, "
    "or treat an explicitly inconclusive branch as a false finding. "
    "Attribution objections must identify a materially false claim or misleading omission. Accurate "
    "scope stated in metadata counts; require body placement only under an explicit obligation. "
    "For a research gap, identify the asserted technical claim requiring evidence, not merely a "
    "requested deliverable absent from a source. When an authored procedure, control or evidence "
    "check can be designed from supported facts, retain it as an authoring obligation. Conditions "
    "to establish during future application belong in future_validation. Do not introduce a "
    "specific identity-binding mechanism when the request only asks for generic identity binding. "
    "Keep genuine unsupported factual mechanisms and contradictions blocking; source relevance "
    "or authority alone does not establish adequate factual support. "
)
