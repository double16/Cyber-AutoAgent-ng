## What still failed

Network's assessor proposed that the documented policy could be stale while enforcement had
legitimately changed. Its initial response marked a matching permitted counterexample without an
excluding control while agreeing with approval, so Python correctly rejected that inconsistent
combination. The assessor's correction changed to disagreement and demanded policy-currency
verification. The shared allowance was then exhausted before a critic response to that dispute.

The purported permitted scenario is questionable: the method requires documented intended policy
to be established before interpretation. A stale document does not by itself establish that the
new enforcement is authorized, and defining intended policy by whatever enforcement currently does
would be circular. Independent confirmation of approved policy may improve a procedure, but the
assessor did not convincingly establish a material defect under the human's acceptable label.
Do not claim the typed fields solved this semantic problem; they solved only the applicability
shape of non-boundary analysis.

Filesystem's first critic approved the document and considered generic object/path language
sufficient. The assessor disagreed, correctly identifying the clarified file-access requirement,
but also repeated the disputed literal-source permission-denial objection. The corrected critic
reported both issues while returning no interpretations and no no-security-checks explanation.
That violated the unchanged complete-review contract. With correction exhausted, no valid final
decision was published. The real clarity defect was recognized, but the workflow could not complete.

## Implications

Adding explicit purpose improved one narrow reasoning distinction without establishing reliable
human-aligned review. More schema requirements and repeated full output may also burden corrections,
but this trial does not isolate that cause. A missing correction table is a contract failure, not
proof of wrong authoring; an optional hypothetical is not automatically a material document defect.

Do not immediately spend another allowance on stronger wording. First examine a smaller correction
contract that retains controller-owned unchanged subject evidence and requires explicit changes to
disputed judgments, rather than regenerating the entire interpretation table. Preserve semantic
judgment with the model and never silently reuse a disputed interpretation as approved. A deliberate
human-adjudication path for unresolved reviews is another policy option; it would need explicit
workflow design and authorization, not automatic publication of disputed drafts.

No production readiness or generation improvement is established by this run. All failed cases
remain failed, and the remaining three requests were not used to restart either decision.
